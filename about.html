THE STUDIO
BEHIND THE GAMES

We're a small independent studio building focused, replayable mobile games and apps. We favor clear design and responsive feel over noise — small scopes, careful craft, and titles that are easy to pick up and hard to put down.

See our titles Get in touch

How we got here.

Caulderon Interactive started with a simple constraint: build a game without leaning on a stock-art marketplace, a sample pack, or a third-party engine's built-in content. Number Run: Pro Edition was the first result — a fast-paced number game where every orb skin, world background, and sound effect is generated by our own code the moment it's needed, not loaded from a file someone else made.

That constraint turned out to be worth keeping. Tilt Maze and Block Chakra followed the same rule, each with its own mechanics and its own palette. MeritNiti: Exam Practice, a quiz app for competitive exam prep, and Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen, a dark fantasy adventure, are both currently in development, built the same way.

We're not trying to be a large studio. We'd rather ship a handful of titles we're genuinely proud of than spread a bigger team thin across many mediocre ones.

What guides the work.

A few working principles show up in every title, whether it's shipped or still in progress.

Craft over shortcuts

Art and audio are written in code rather than bought or borrowed. It's slower, but it means every visual and sound is something we actually made, and can change freely without licensing constraints.

Respect for your data

Our apps don't ask for accounts. Progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device by default, and we keep the list of third-party services we rely on short and disclosed.

Small, sustainable scope

Each title ships independently, with its own mechanics and its own timeline. We'd rather take longer on fewer things than rush several at once.

Honest monetization

Where our apps carry ads or purchases, we favor plain, well-labeled options — like a single one-time purchase to remove ads — over pressure tactics or pay-to-win mechanics.

Built on a small, deliberate stack.

Our Android titles share a common technical foundation. Sticking to a small, well-understood toolchain is part of how a small team keeps every title maintainable.

01
Kotlin & Jetpack ComposeOur apps are written in Kotlin using Jetpack Compose and AndroidX, both under the Apache License 2.0.
02
Google Play BillingIn-app purchases, where they exist, are processed entirely through Google Play Billing — we never handle payment details ourselves.
03
Google AdMob & UMPAdvertising, where present, is served through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with consent managed through Google's User Messaging Platform.
04
Canvas, not asset packsVisuals are drawn with Compose's Canvas APIs and audio is synthesized at runtime — the only "assets" are the algorithms that generate them.
