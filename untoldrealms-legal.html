AI-Generated / AI-Assisted Art

The current release includes locally bundled AI-generated or AI-assisted scene art and character portraits. These visuals are used selectively for important establishing shots, characters, revelations, combat, and finales. Local Audio

The App bundles local WAV ambience, music, UI, and combat audio and plays it with Android media APIs. It does not stream audio or contact an online music service.
