Overview

This policy explains how Eltru's Way Home: Mini Games (the “App”), developed by Caulderon Interactive, handles information. It applies to the Android package com.caulderoninteractive.tiltmaze.

Information We Receive Directly

The App does not require a Caulderon account or ask you to submit a profile to play. Caulderon Interactive does not operate a gameplay server that receives your progress, maze movement, mini-game decisions or camp state. If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use it to respond.

Local Game Data

Game progress, mode history, rewards, settings, gifts and customisation choices may be stored in the App's private storage on your device. Clearing App storage or uninstalling can remove local progress. We cannot restore data that was never sent to us.

Motion Controls

Tilt-maze activities can read the device accelerometer or gravity sensor in real time to control movement. The App uses those readings for gameplay and does not use them to determine your physical location or transmit a movement history to Caulderon Interactive.

Ads, Purchases and Service Providers

The Google Play release contains advertising and may offer optional in-app purchases. Google Play processes payments; Caulderon Interactive does not receive your full payment-card details. Google and advertising or consent services can process limited device, app, ad-interaction, purchase or fraud-prevention information under their own policies to provide those functions. Availability and personalisation can depend on region, age settings and consent.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms.

Permissions and Device Features

The App may use internet and network-state access for ads, purchases and store services; haptic feedback; and motion sensors for tilt gameplay. It does not need access to your contacts, microphone or precise location for its core mini-games.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and should be used under the supervision and account settings appropriate to the player's age. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly to Caulderon Interactive through the App. Parents or guardians may contact us with a privacy concern.

Your Choices

  • Use device or in-App privacy options to manage advertising consent where available.
  • Review or cancel purchases through Google Play under Google's policies.
  • Clear local game data through Android App Info or uninstall the App.
  • Contact us to ask about information you previously sent to support.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the App, its providers or applicable requirements change. The current date will appear above. Privacy questions may be sent to support@caulderoninteractive.com.
