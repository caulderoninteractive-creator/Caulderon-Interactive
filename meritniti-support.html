Download and Update

Install or update MeritNiti from Google Play. If the latest release does not appear, restart the Play Store, check the selected account and make sure the device has free storage.

Practice Progress or Bookmarks Disappeared

Practice attempts and preferences are stored locally. Check whether App storage was cleared, the App was uninstalled, a work profile changed or Android restored another device state. We cannot retrieve practice history from a server because MeritNiti does not operate an account-based cloud save.

Government-Job Updates

Open the Government Jobs & Exam Updates board for current verified coverage. Each listing links to official material. If a summary and authority notice differ, follow the authority.

Report a Correction or Missing Update

Email the MeritNiti page URL, the official authority URL and a short description of the issue. Do not send application credentials, Aadhaar, payment details or other sensitive documents. Listings can be temporarily removed while an official source is checked.

Purchase Help

Google Play processes payments and refunds. Keep the Play receipt, relaunch the App while connected and use Google Play purchase history or support for payment disputes. Do not send your full payment-card number.

Contact Support

Email support@caulderoninteractive.com. For an App issue, include the version, Android version, device model and steps to reproduce. For a listing correction, include the MeritNiti and official-source URLs.

You can also follow the MeritNiti WhatsApp channel for published updates.

MeritNiti is independent and is not affiliated with a government agency or recruiting authority.
