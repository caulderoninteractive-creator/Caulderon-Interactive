TILT MAZE

Steady hands. Quick heart.

A physics-driven ball maze controlled entirely by the tilt of your phone. Calibrate in a three-count, roll through hand-crafted mazes, skirt the pits, and chase gold-star best times — one more run at a time.

Coming soon to Google Play
Free to play, with an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Every angle is a decision.

Tilt Maze reads your phone's gravity sensor in real time — the angle you hold at becomes "level," and every lean rolls the ball. Thread narrow corridors, skim past crimson pits, and reach the glowing goal. Fall in, and the run's streak burns to ash.

True tilt steeringGravity-sensor control with a three-count calibration, plus invert and sensitivity tuning in Settings.

Dug-in pitsProcedurally-jittered hazards with a fall-in animation, near-miss detection, and screen-shake impact feedback.

Explore modeOptional fog of war hides the maze until you physically roll through it — pure exploration, no map memory.

What's inside.

Progress lives on your device: coins, star-rated best times, achievements, and your perfect hot-streak runs.

Hot streaks

Consecutive pit-free clears build a streak that pays out bonus coins — and vanishes the instant you fall.

Procedural music

Calm and focused adaptive music beds with smooth crossfades, plus optional haptic feedback throughout.

One-time Remove Ads

A single non-consumable purchase removes banner and interstitial ads, processed by Google Play Billing.

Local-only progress

Coins, times, achievements and settings stay on your device. No account, no sign-up, no cloud sync.

Accessibility fallbacks

An on-screen direction pad appears automatically on devices without a usable motion sensor.

Privacy-first sensing

The motion sensor is read only to steer gameplay in real time — that data is never stored or sent anywhere.
