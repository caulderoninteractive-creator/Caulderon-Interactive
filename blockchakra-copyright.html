Art & Visual Assets

All visual elements in Block Chakra — tiles, board patterns, UI, and effects — are drawn programmatically on Canvas at runtime. No third-party stock art, bitmap image assets, or third-party fonts are used.

Audio

Music and sound effects in Block Chakra are procedurally synthesized at runtime through the application's own code. No third-party audio files, stock sound effects, or externally sourced recordings are used.
