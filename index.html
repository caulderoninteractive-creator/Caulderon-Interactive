Nothing in these games came from a stock library.

Every tile, icon, and background is drawn in code at runtime. Every note of music and every sound effect is synthesized, not sampled. It's a harder way to build a game — and it's the only way we build ours.

More about the studio
01
Programmatic visualsEvery tile, icon, and backdrop is drawn on-canvas at runtime — no bitmap art, no stock illustration.
02
Synthesized audioMusic and sound effects are generated as raw waveforms in code, not licensed or sampled from anywhere.
03
Store assets, tooEven our Play Store icons and screenshots are produced with scripts rather than design software.
