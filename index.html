Nothing here came from a stock library.

Every tile, icon, and background across our games is drawn in code at runtime. Every note of music and every sound effect is synthesized, not sampled. It's a harder way to build software — and it's the only way we build ours.

More about the studio
01
Programmatic visualsEvery tile, icon, and backdrop is drawn on-canvas at runtime — no bitmap art, no stock illustration.

Small team. Deliberate scope.

Caulderon Interactive is run as a small, focused operation — one title at a time gets the attention it needs, rather than several getting half of it. We favor clear design and responsive feel over noise, and we'd rather ship four games we're proud of than forty we're not.
