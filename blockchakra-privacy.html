Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Chakra ("the App," "we," "us") handles information when you use the App. Block Chakra is developed by Caulderon Interactive.

Information We Collect Directly

The App does not require an account, login, or sign-up. We do not directly collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier. Gameplay progress — including your best score, coins, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device only, and is not transmitted to any server we operate.

Device Sensors

The App reads your device's motion/gravity sensor to let you control the board's gravity by tilting your phone. This sensor data is used only in real time to control gameplay and is never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays banner and interstitial ads.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — manages advertising consent for users in the EEA, UK, and other regions with applicable requirements.

Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions Used By This App

  • Vibrate — for haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Internet / Network State — required to load ads and process purchases.
  • Billing — required to process the optional "Remove Ads" purchase.
  • Motion sensor (accelerometer/gravity) — controls the board's gravity; reading it requires no runtime permission on Android and no data ever leaves your device.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Data Storage & Security

Gameplay data (best score, coins, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Block Chakra is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region).

Your Choices & Rights

  • Ad personalization consent: EEA/UK users can review or withdraw consent any time via Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You may delete all locally stored App data any time by uninstalling the App.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here and at this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@caulderoninteractive.com.
