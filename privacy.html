Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Number Run: Pro Edition ("the App") handles information when you use the App. Number Run: Pro Edition is developed by Caulderon Interactive.

Information We Collect Directly

The App does not require an account, login, or sign-up. We do not directly collect your name, email address, or other personal identifier. Gameplay progress — including score, Coins, purchases, achievements, and settings — is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to any server we operate. The timestamp of your last session is also stored locally and is used only to time occasional in-app and local notification reminders after you have been away for a while.

Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — displays banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. It may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID, approximate location and app interaction data to serve and measure advertising. A rewarded video may occasionally play more than one short video back-to-back before the reward is granted.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the one-time Remove Ads purchase, consumable Coin Packs, and consumable Combo Bundles containing Coins plus permanent cosmetic/world unlocks and/or Remove Ads. Purchase information is handled directly by Google Play; we do not receive or store payment-card details, and we do not operate a backend server that independently verifies or records individual purchase transactions.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — obtains and manages advertising consent for users in applicable regions. You can review or change consent through Settings → Privacy Options when available.

We do not use other analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs beyond those described above. Google's Privacy Policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

Permissions Used By This App

  • Vibrate — haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Internet / Network State — required to load ads, process purchases and manage advertising consent.
  • Billing — required to process optional in-app purchases.
  • Notifications — used for occasional local comeback reminders. These are generated entirely on your device; no push notification service or third party is involved.

The App does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files, and does not use any such permission.

Data Storage & Security

Gameplay data is stored on your device. Automatic Android device backup is disabled for the App, so this local data is not included in standard cloud device backups. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Number Run: Pro Edition is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your region) and is not designed as a Families-policy children's app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices & Rights

  • EEA/UK users and other users in regions with applicable consent requirements can review or withdraw advertising consent through Settings → Privacy Options.
  • You can reset or limit advertising tracking through your device settings.
  • You can disable comeback notifications in your device settings for the App; this does not affect other App features.
  • You may delete locally stored App data by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android Settings.
  • Depending on your location, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA regarding information held by third-party service providers. Because we do not operate our own data servers, such requests are generally best directed to the relevant provider, or to us and we will assist where we can.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact support@caulderoninteractive.com.
