Overview

This policy explains how MeritNiti: Exam Practice (the “App”) and the associated MeritNiti website, including Government Jobs & Exam Updates (together, the “Services”), handle information. MeritNiti is operated by Caulderon Interactive.

Practice Data Stored on Your Device

The App does not require a Caulderon account. Question attempts, selected answers, bookmarks, weak-area or misconception tags derived from attempts, revision lists and display preferences are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to a gameplay server operated by us. Clearing App storage or uninstalling can remove them.

Website and Government-Jobs Board

The website does not require an account and does not accept job applications. Hosting and security providers may process standard web-request information such as IP address, browser or user-agent information, requested URLs and security metadata as necessary to deliver and protect the site. Search and filter choices on the jobs board are handled in your browser and can appear in the page URL when you choose to share it.

Official Links and External Sites

Links to recruiting authorities, application portals, notices, Google Play and WhatsApp open third-party services. Information you submit there is governed by those services and is not received by MeritNiti through the link itself. Never send application credentials or payment-card details to MeritNiti support.

Google Play and Purchases

The released App may offer optional in-app purchases processed by Google Play. Google handles payment credentials; we do not receive your full payment-card number. Google and platform services may process device, purchase, fraud-prevention and service metadata under their policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App may use internet and network access for Play services, content updates or purchases. It does not need your contacts, microphone, camera or precise location for core exam-practice functions.

Retention and Your Choices

Local practice data remains until App storage is cleared or the App is uninstalled. Support emails remain as needed to answer and maintain a record of the request. You may contact us about information you sent directly. Data submitted to an external authority or platform must be managed with that provider.

Children's Privacy

MeritNiti is intended for users preparing for competitive and recruitment exams and is not directed at children under 13 or the applicable minimum age. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information directly through the App.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy when the Services or applicable requirements change. Privacy questions may be sent to support@caulderoninteractive.com.

This policy covers MeritNiti's Android practice app and website. The official privacy terms of linked authorities and platforms apply on their services.
