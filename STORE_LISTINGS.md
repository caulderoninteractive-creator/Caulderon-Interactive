## Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen

**App name (28/30):** Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen

**Short description (77/80):** Explore Greyfen, master brutal combat, and decide the fate of a broken crown.

**Full description:**

Some crowns are inherited. Some are taken. A few should be destroyed.

Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen is an offline dark-fantasy action RPG set across the Greyfen Marches — a border realm living beneath the official history of a dead king and a Regency that would rather the past stay buried.

Create your character, cross a large connected world, enter ruined chapels and oath-crypts, fight soldiers, raiders and bosses, gather evidence, build your reputation, and decide what the Ashen Crown should mean when the truth finally reaches Crownwatch.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore a 164×112 exterior world with 15 named landmarks, settlements, rivers, roads and hidden routes
• Enter four authored mission interiors: Greyfen Chapel, Ashen Monastery, Mirewatch Oath-Crypt and the Crownwatch Black Vault
• Fight in real time with light and heavy attacks, stamina, blocking, perfect parries and dodges
• Switch between stable top-down exploration and an optional first-person view
• Choose from five origins and customize hair, eyes, complexion and scars
• Equip weapons and armour, manage inventory, trade with merchants and craft useful supplies
• Track hunger, rest, XP, levels, quests, discoveries and reputation
• Follow branching conversations, witnesses and political choices that alter how Greyfen remembers you
• Reach three distinct endings — then continue exploring after the conclusion
• Play fully offline with no account required

THE WORLD REMEMBERS
Quest state, allegiances, reputation and key decisions persist in your local save. The story is not only about defeating enemies; it is about deciding whether authority deserves restoration, reform, or destruction.

BUILT FOR ANDROID
Ashen Crown is a native Android RPG with touch movement, phone-readable UI, 30/60 FPS options, autosaving, manual saves, haptics, local audio and a recovery path for unexpected runtime errors.

Current build: no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no account system.

## Untold Realms

**App name (13/30):** Untold Realms

**Short description (71/80):** Six worlds. 280 scenes. Tactical RPG choices that reshape every ending.

**Full description:**

One engine. Six worlds. No single “right” ending.

Untold Realms is an offline anthology RPG built around novella-scale campaigns, persistent choices, tactical encounters, relationships, faction politics, investigation and multiple endings. Each campaign has its own setting, tone, conflicts and consequences, while a shared RPG engine keeps choices, inventory, quests, companions and state meaningful.

SIX CAMPAIGNS
• The Ashen Crown — dark fantasy, ruined succession and dangerous oaths
• The Hollow Orbit — science fiction across a shattered orbital ring and sentient translation network
• Dust Remnant — post-apocalyptic water politics, convoy crimes and buried infrastructure
• Neon Covenant — cyberpunk identity noir about memory ownership and corporate citizenship
• The Hollow Saint — gothic occult horror built around plague history, false records and inherited guilt
• Velvet Ashes — political romance and tragedy during a constitutional crisis

A LARGE BRANCHING ANTHOLOGY
The current v0.8 build contains 280 reachable scenes, 898 authored choices, more than 61,000 story words, 60 quests and 36 endings.

CHOICES CHANGE SYSTEMS
Your decisions can alter evidence, trust, affection, faction standing, inventory, companions, later routes, dialogue and ending availability. Endings emerge from accumulated state rather than a single final button.

MORE THAN TEXT
Untold Realms combines text-first storytelling with selective scene art and portraits, tactical combat, items, quests, faction and relationship systems, campaign/origin selection, ambient audio and campaign-specific presentation.

SAVE YOUR CHRONICLE
Consequential actions autosave. Three manual Chronicle slots let you preserve alternate paths for long campaigns.

PLAY OFFLINE
No account is required. The current Android build has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no network permission.
