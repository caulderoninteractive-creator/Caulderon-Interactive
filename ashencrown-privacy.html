This page describes our current plan for Ashen Crown, drafted before the game has shipped. It follows the same approach as our other titles — no accounts, local-only storage, and a short list of disclosed third-party services — but it will be reviewed and finalized to match the game's actual feature set before launch.

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen ("the App," "we," "us") is planned to handle information when you use the App. Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen is developed by Caulderon Interactive.

Information We Collect Directly

As planned, the App will not require an account, login, or sign-up. We do not intend to directly collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier. Quest progress — including your character, inventory, world state, and settings — is intended to be stored locally on your device only, and not transmitted to any server we operate.

Third-Party Services

If the App carries advertising or purchases, we expect to rely on the same third-party services used across our other titles, each of which collects information according to its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — would display banner, interstitial, and/or rewarded ads, and may collect device identifiers such as the Advertising ID, approximate location, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Play Billing — would process any optional purchase, such as consumable Sigil packs or a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Purchase information would be handled directly by Google Play; we would not receive or store your payment details.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — would manage advertising consent for users in the EEA, UK, and other regions with applicable requirements.

Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Permissions Used By This App

  • Vibrate — expected to be used for haptic feedback during combat, if included.
  • Internet / Network State — expected to be required to load ads and process any purchases.
  • Billing — required only if an optional in-app purchase is included.

We do not plan for the App to request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Data Storage & Security

Save data (character, inventory, world state, settings) is intended to be stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App would remove this locally stored data.

Children's Privacy

Ashen Crown: Oath of Greyfen is intended for users aged 13 and above (or the relevant minimum age in your region) and is not being designed as a "Families"-policy children's app. We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Ad personalization consent: EEA/UK users would be able to review or withdraw consent via Settings → Privacy Options, if advertising is included.
  • You would be able to delete all locally stored App data at any time by uninstalling the App.

Changes To This Policy

This draft will be reviewed and finalized before Ashen Crown launches, and may change as the game's feature set is finalized. Changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date once the App is live.

Contact Us

Questions about this draft policy can be sent to support.caulderoninteractive@gmail.com.
