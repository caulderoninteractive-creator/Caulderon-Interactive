ELTRU'S
WAY HOME

A little traveller. A world of play.

Guide Eltru through magical tilt mazes and a growing storybook of mini-games. Follow the Journey, test Mastery, explore Expeditions, return for rotating challenges, and make camp between adventures.

Available on Google Play
Free to download · Contains ads and optional in-app purchases

More than a maze.

Eltru's Way Home grows the original tilt-maze idea into a varied, friendly adventure. Motion-led mazes sit beside match-three, memory, snakes-and-ladders-style boards and other short-form challenges, joined by a story journey and a home base that makes every return feel personal.
