ASHEN CROWN
OATH OF GREYFEN

Some oaths outlive kingdoms.

A dark fantasy adventure through the ruins of Greyfen, where a broken crown and an old oath refuse to stay buried. Exploration, tactical battles, and consequences that follow you — built the same code-first way as every Caulderon Interactive title.

In development Not yet available. Want to know when it launches? Email us and we'll let you know.

A kingdom worth returning to.

Ashen Crown is early in development, so specifics may still change — but the shape of it is set: a world worth exploring slowly, battles that reward planning over grinding, and a story that remembers what you did.

01
A broken kingdom to exploreHand-crafted regions across the ruins of Greyfen, uncovered as you travel rather than handed to you on a map.
02
Tactical battlesPositioning and timing are meant to matter more than raw stats — encounters designed to be planned, not just powered through.
03
An oath with weightChoices are intended to trace back to the oath at the center of the story, shaping how the kingdom's factions treat you.
04
Growth without grindCharacters and gear are meant to develop through play, not through waiting timers or paywalled shortcuts.

Built the way we build everything.

Even a fantasy world follows studio tradition: no bitmap art, no licensed audio. Details below reflect current plans and are subject to change as the game takes final shape.

Code-drawn world

Even Greyfen's linework and lighting are planned to be generated in code — consistent with every other Caulderon Interactive title.

Procedural score

An adaptive dark-fantasy score and sound design, synthesized at runtime rather than licensed or sampled.

No account required

Plans call for the same local-first approach as our other titles — no sign-up to start playing.

Local-only saves

Quest progress, characters, and settings are intended to stay on your device by default.

Simple, honest monetization

If ads or purchases are included, we plan to follow the same pattern as our other titles: clearly labeled, with a one-time option to remove ads.

No pay-to-win

Any optional purchases are intended to be cosmetic or convenience-only — never a shortcut past the game's actual challenge.

Because the game hasn't shipped yet, the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use below describe our current plans and will be finalized before launch.
