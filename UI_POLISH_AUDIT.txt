STORE ICONS
Google Play / App Store badges were plain centered text with no iconography.
Rebuilt as icon + two-line label components:
  - A generic "play" glyph for Google Play, a generic rounded-square /
    arrow glyph for the App Store — deliberately NOT reproductions of
    Google's or Apple's actual trademarked marks (same approach the old
    README.txt flagged: placeholder-style glyphs meant to stand in until
    official badge artwork is dropped in).

    to Tilt Maze / Block Chakra ("coming soon to Google Play").
  - MeritNiti and the two RPGs weren't given store badges — they don't
    have a public store listing yet, so nothing was invented for them.
