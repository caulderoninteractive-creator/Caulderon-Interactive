Who can apply
For Personal and Senior Personal Assistant- Graduate with Shorthand speed of 100 words per minute and typing speed of 40 words per minute and having knowledge of computer. For Peon - Matriculation pass or equivalent from a recognized board / Higher Secondary with driving licence of LMV and 2 years unblemished driving experience.
Pay / salary
Pay Level 3 Basic Pay ₹21,700 per month to Pay Level 8 Basic Pay ₹47,600 per month
Age limit
Must have attained the age of 18 years but should not have attained the age of 27 years (Age will be reckoned as on 01/06/2026)
Application fee
PAYMENT: ₹ 100/- (One Hundred only) (i) Women candidates and candidates belonging to Schedule Caste, Schedule Tribe, PwBD (Person with Benchmark Disability) & Ex-serviceman category are exempted from paying
How selection works
Tier 1(Objective) Tier 2(Skill Test) Tier 3(Interview)
