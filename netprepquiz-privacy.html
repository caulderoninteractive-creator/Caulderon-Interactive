NetPrepQuiz has not launched. The statements below describe the present product plan, not a currently distributed build. This page will be checked against the final app and Google Play Data safety declaration before release.

Scope

This draft explains how NetPrepQuiz (the “App”), developed by Caulderon Interactive, is intended to handle information. It applies only to NetPrepQuiz and not to other Caulderon Interactive products.

Local Data

The planned first release will not require an account. Practice progress, scores, streaks, bookmarks, and preferences are intended to remain on the user’s device. Caulderon Interactive will not operate a server that receives this local practice history unless the product is changed and this policy is updated first.

Clearing app storage or uninstalling the App may permanently remove locally stored information.

Third-Party Services

If the final App includes Google Play Billing, Google Play services, crash reporting, or Google Mobile Ads, those providers may process device, diagnostic, purchase, advertising, or interaction information under their own terms and privacy policies. Any enabled service will be identified here and in the final Google Play Data safety declaration before launch.

Caulderon Interactive will not receive payment-card details processed by Google Play.

Permissions

The final Android manifest will define the permissions the App uses. NetPrepQuiz will request only permissions needed for released features, and this section will be updated if notification, network, advertising, or other permissions are included.

Children’s Privacy

The App is planned as a general exam-preparation tool and is not designed to solicit personal information from children. If we learn that personal information was provided to us directly by a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Your Choices

You can stop all local processing by uninstalling the App. Where applicable, Android and Google settings will control notifications, advertising choices, and purchases. Privacy enquiries may be sent to the contact below.

Contact

Email caulderoninteractive@gmail.com with “NetPrepQuiz privacy” in the subject line.

This is a pre-release draft and is not a substitute for the final policy that will accompany the distributed app.
