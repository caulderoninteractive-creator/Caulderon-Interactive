NUMBER RUN
PRO EDITION

Think fast. Run faster.

A fast-paced number challenge built around split-second decisions. Run through gates that add, subtract, multiply, and divide your value, land on the exact target, and push every run a little further than the last.

Available on Google Play
Free to play, with optional Coin purchases and a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Every gate is a decision. Every level has one exact answer.

Number Run is an exact-target game — each level has a precise numeric goal, and every gate does exactly what it says. Nothing is fudged mid-run, which means the skill is entirely in reading the run ahead of you.

01
Split-second arithmeticGates add, subtract, multiply, or divide your running value. Land it on the level's exact target to clear.
02
Nine hazard modifiers, one rewardFrozen, Heat, Gravity Shift, Current, Snare, Volatile, Mirror, Toxic, and Chaos gates complicate a run — and the rare MYSTERY gate occasionally rewards one instead.
03
Multiplier FatigueChaining multiply gates back-to-back earns diminishing score returns, so runaway combos stay interesting without ever changing what a gate actually does.
04
The PortalA 50-shard mosaic fills in over many runs — each shard a uniquely shaped, procedurally generated piece of the whole.

Built to reward one more run.

Like every Caulderon Interactive title, every pixel and every sound in Number Run is produced in code.

Double or Nothing

Risk part of a run's Coins for a chance to double them — pure optional upside, never required, and never worth more than the run you're already having.

Fully procedural audio

Every music loop and sound effect is synthesized at runtime — no sampled or licensed audio anywhere in the app.

No account required

Score, Coins, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing to sign up for.

Coins & achievements

Earn Coins from every run and unlock achievements as your lifetime stats grow, or top up with optional Coin Packs.

Reasonable monetization

A single non-consumable purchase removes ads for good. Coin Packs and Combo Bundles are entirely optional.

Privacy-first by design

We don't collect your name, email, or any personal identifier. See the full Privacy Policy for specifics.
