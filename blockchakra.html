BLOCK CHAKRA

Spin gravity. Clear the grid.

Swipe the board in any direction and every tile falls that way, like gravity just flipped. Fill a full row or column to clear it, chain cascades for bigger combos, and keep the board under control before it overflows.

Coming soon to Google Play
Free to play, with an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.

Four directions. One board. No two runs alike.

Swipe is the default control and works the same on every device. Tilt is there too, entirely optional, for players who'd rather steer gravity by turning the phone in their hand.

The board keeps refilling after every move, so the real skill is staying ahead of it — clearing lines faster than new tiles arrive, and reading the danger meter before it reads you.

Built to feel warm, not sharp.

Block Chakra trades the neon arcade look for a bright, hand-tuned "carnival" palette — and like every Caulderon Interactive title, every pixel of it is drawn in code.

Four board patterns

Dots, diamonds, chevron, or sunburst — pick a backdrop from Settings, all drawn live with plain canvas shapes.

Fully procedural audio

Two music loops and six sound effects, all synthesized at runtime — no sampled or licensed audio anywhere.

No account required

Best score, coins, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing to sign up for.

Reliable tilt input

Lifecycle-aware sensor handling with automatic recalibration, so tilt controls stay responsive through long sessions.

Free, with one purchase

The game is free to play. A single optional purchase removes ads for good — no subscriptions, no loot boxes.

Privacy-first by design

The motion sensor is read only to steer gameplay in real time — that data is never stored or sent anywhere.
