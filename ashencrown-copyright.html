Art & Visual Assets

Consistent with every Caulderon Interactive title, Ashen Crown's world, characters, and UI are planned to be drawn programmatically on Canvas at runtime, rather than imported from bitmap art, stock illustration, or third-party fonts.

Audio

Music and sound effects are planned to be procedurally synthesized at runtime through the application's own code. No third-party audio files, stock sound effects, or externally sourced recordings are planned to be used.
