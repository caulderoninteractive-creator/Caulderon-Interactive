MERITNITI
EXAM PRACTICE

Strategy behind every selection.

Every selection should teach you something. MeritNiti is an independent competitive-exam practice platform designed to make practice diagnostic rather than repetitive — built to help you understand the reasoning behind an answer, recognize recurring mistakes, and turn weak areas into focused revision.

Learn from the answer you actually chose.

A wrong answer is often useful evidence about how you're thinking. MeritNiti is built around that idea, rather than stopping at a plain "correct" or "incorrect."

01

Practice → Understand → Improve.

MeritNiti isn't meant to make preparation an exercise in endless tapping. Its purpose is to help you notice:

Why did this option seem correct?

Distractor-specific feedback addresses the reasoning behind the wrong choice, not just the right one.

What part of the concept did I misunderstand?

Full solutions connect the answer back to the underlying concept, not just the correct letter.

Is this mistake recurring?

Misconception tags link individual errors to patterns that show up across sessions.

What should I revise next?

Smart Revision turns that pattern into a prioritized, ready-to-study list.
