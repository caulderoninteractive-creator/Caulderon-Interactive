How MeritNiti treats job information.

Official-source-first

Listings link to the recruiting authority or authorised application source, not an anonymous repost.

Freshness gates

Expired, unreachable or insufficiently verified rows leave the active board until checked again.

No application collection

MeritNiti does not accept job applications or credentials. Applying takes place on the authority's portal.
