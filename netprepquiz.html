NETPREPQUIZ

Practice with purpose.

A focused quiz app for exam and certification prep — timed practice sets, instant scoring, and clear progress tracking, built the same code-first way as every Caulderon Interactive title.

In development Not yet available. Want to know when it launches? Email us and we'll let you know.

Practice sets that respect your time.

NetPrepQuiz is early in development, so specifics may still change — but the direction is set: short, focused practice sessions with immediate, honest feedback, rather than an endless scroll of unrelated questions.

01
Timed practice setsShort, focused rounds you can fit into a break, rather than one long undifferentiated test bank.
02
Instant scoring & explanationsKnow immediately whether an answer was right, and why — not just a final score at the end.
03
Organized by topicQuestions grouped by subject and difficulty, so you can target the areas that actually need work.
04
Progress you can seeTrack accuracy and streaks over time, so improvement is visible, not just assumed.

Built the way we build everything.

Even in development, NetPrepQuiz follows the same studio-wide defaults as our other titles — subject to change as the app takes final shape.

No account required

Plans call for the same local-first approach as our other apps — no sign-up just to start practicing.

Local-only progress

Scores and streaks are intended to stay on your device by default, with no account needed to track them.

Code-drawn interface

Consistent with every Caulderon Interactive title, the interface is built and drawn from our own code rather than a template UI kit.

Simple, honest monetization

If ads or purchases are included, we plan to follow the same pattern as our other titles: clearly labeled, with a one-time option to remove ads.

Because the app hasn't shipped yet, the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use below describe our current plans and will be finalized before launch.
