Support information for the current offline anthology build, including Chronicle saves, six campaigns, and bug-report details.

Does Untold Realms need internet?

No. The current Android build is fully playable offline and does not request the Android Internet permission.

What campaigns are included?

The v0.8 build includes six campaigns: The Ashen Crown, The Hollow Orbit, Dust Remnant, Neon Covenant, The Hollow Saint, and Velvet Ashes.

How does saving work?

Consequential actions are autosaved. The Save panel also provides three separate manual Chronicle slots (Save 1–3 / Load 1–3).

My progress disappeared

The current version has no Caulderon account/cloud-save service. Check whether App data was cleared, the App was reinstalled, or Android restored a different device backup. Local progress cannot be recovered from our servers because we do not possess a server copy.

Do choices really matter?

Yes. Choices can modify evidence, relationships, faction standing, inventory, route access, later dialogue, and ending availability. The current validated build contains 280 reachable scenes, 898 authored choices, 60 quests, and 36 endings across six campaigns.

Are campaigns or endings sold separately?

No. The current v0.8 build has no ads, in-app purchases, paid choices, or subscriptions.

A screen or choice is not updating correctly

Restart the App first. If the issue repeats, note the campaign, scene or choice text, your Android version, device model, and App version. The current build includes a navigation/state-snapshot fix intended to prevent stale Compose screens; precise reproduction steps are especially useful for regressions.

Contact Support

Email support@caulderoninteractive.com. Include “Untold Realms” in the subject plus the campaign name, game version, Android version, and device model when reporting a bug.
